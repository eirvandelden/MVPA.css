# Intent: Solunized light gets the Solarized cream background

Author: Etienne van Delden de la Haije. Status: accepted. Type: bugfix.
Delivery: autonomous
Codex review: no

## Problem

`solunized-light` and `solunized-white` look the same.
Both use a cool blue-gray page background: light is `oklch(93.79% 0.0095 222.06)`, white is `oklch(95.64% 0.0079 216.62)`.
Light has none of the yellowish cream that Solarized light is known for.

## Proposed outcome

`solunized-light` has the Solarized cream background and `solunized-white` stays the neutral variant, so the two are clearly different.
The default `:root` light palette (a light OS with no `data-theme`) matches `solunized-light`.
The stale "Generated by kyiv" markers are gone, because kyiv was an AI agent and the files are edited by hand.

## Affected users and systems

- Every app that pins mvpa.css and shows the default light theme or `data-theme="solunized-light"`.
- `app/assets/stylesheets/mvpa/4_theme/0_colors.css` and the flattened manifest `app/assets/stylesheets/mvpa/mvpa.css`.
- `test/mvpa_manifest_sync_test.rb`, which checks both files stay in sync.
- `CHANGELOG.md`.

## Constraints

- Edit both files by hand; `mvpa.css` is not generated.
- Keep `bg-0` (cards, sidebar), `bg-1` (page canvas) and `bg-2` (borders) distinguishable, as the existing comment on the bg scale describes.
- Text on the new cream backgrounds keeps readable contrast.
- No new dependency, migration or deploy file is needed, so no extra permission is needed.

## In scope

- Cream values for `--color-bg-0`, `--color-bg-1` and `--color-bg-2` in the `[data-theme="solunized-light"]` block and the default `:root` block, in both files.
- Removing every "Generated by kyiv" comment, and the "generated by kyiv" banner line, in both files.
- A CHANGELOG entry with no `### Upgrading` section, because apps need to do nothing.

## Out of scope

- The white, dark and black themes.
- The foreground, accent and bright (`br-`) colors.
- The eink theme.
- Any change to the structure of the bg scale.

## Acceptance criteria

- With `data-theme="solunized-light"`, the page background is a visibly yellowish cream and no longer blue-gray.
- With `data-theme="solunized-white"`, the page background is unchanged.
- A light OS with no `data-theme` shows the same cream as `solunized-light`.
- Cards and the sidebar are lighter than the page, and borders are darker than the page.
- Body text on the cream page keeps WCAG AA contrast.
- No file mentions kyiv.
- The Minitest suite and all linters in CI pass, including the manifest sync test.

## Decisions

- Hand-edit the palette and remove the kyiv line — "kyiv was an ai agent, remove that line".
- The default `:root` light palette also becomes cream — chosen so it matches `solunized-light`.
- Autonomous delivery without Codex review — chosen at the delivery question.
